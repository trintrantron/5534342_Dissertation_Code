What is a CTF?!

CTF stands for Capture the Flag!

In the world of Cyber Security, a CTF is a collection of challenges where you solve a problem to identify the flag.

Each challenge aims to teach you something different about Cyber Security.

And each flag that you discover earns you points...

------------------------------

What is Cyber Security?

Cyber Security is the practice of protecting computers and other technology from hackers.

Hackers can attept to steal information, break systems or impersonate people online.

Sometimes to protect your systems from hackers, you have to understand how to attack like them...
